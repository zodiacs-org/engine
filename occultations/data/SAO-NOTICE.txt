Stellar source records: Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory Star Catalog
(SAO Staff, 1966), NASA Astronomical Data Center J2000 edition corrected
through May 1991, supplied by NASA HEASARC from ADC/CDS catalogue I/131A.

Catalogue documentation: Nancy G. Roman and Wayne H. Warren Jr.,
Documentation for the Machine-Readable Version, NSSDC/WDC-A-R&S 89-08,
NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. J2000 positions and proper motions were
computed by Clayton A. Smith at the US Naval Observatory.

NASA NTRS classifies the original catalogue and its documentation as works
of the US Government with public use permitted. The original compilation's
source codes are retained. This statement does not apply to other catalogues
hosted by NASA or CDS. SIMBAD-derived magnitude corrections for SAO 84129
and SAO 92548 and imported cross-identification fields are excluded.

Retain sao-documentation.txt with machine-readable distributions, as its
Introduction requests. It is an unchanged copy of the NASA CD-ROM text from:
