sources/selection.json and sources/hyg-selected.json select HIP-backed named
bright-star rows and retain their HYG and HIP identifiers and original CSV
records. src/catalog-data.ts adapts those rows: RA hours become degrees;
the interpreted ICRS directions and tangential proper motions are rotated
through the parent engine's ICRS-to-J2000 bias matrix and motion components
are reprojected onto the rotated tangent bases. The source epoch is retained.
The catalog includes curated aliases and historical attributions as documented
in sources/selection.json. These selected and adapted data are CC BY-SA 4.0,
distinct from MIT code. No endorsement by the source author is implied.

Historical text: Ptolemy, Tetrabiblos, translated by J. M. Ashmand,
London: Davis and Dickson, 1822, Book I, chapters IX to XI.
Public-domain original text; short passages transcribed from the IAPSOP scan.
https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1822__ashmand___ptolemys_tetrabiblos.pdf
Locators and transcription decisions: sources/lore-passages.json.
These are historical beliefs, not statements of physical or predictive facts.

Definitions: ESA, The Hipparcos and Tycho Catalogues, ESA SP-1200,
1997, volume 1, section 1.2. No ESA catalog data are copied here.
https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/cats/more/HIP/cdroms/docs/vol1/sect1_02.pdf
