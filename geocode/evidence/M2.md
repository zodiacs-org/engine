script requires the compiled tools produced by `index:build` and validates
locally produced builds; it is not the future loader for untrusted indexes.

counts, manifest hash, largest shard, transformations, file-limit checks and
byte-identical rebuild. These checks cover exactly the recorded
`cities15000` snapshot and accompanying country and region inputs, not every
GeoNames dump or geographic correctness. A later daily download may differ.
Full downloads and generated indexes remain gitignored.
