## 3.0.0 (cut 2026-11-27)

- **A lighter default (breaking).** The root entry computes on
  astronomy-engine 2.1.19 and loads no DE440 data: an application of
  `natalChart` is 79,787 bytes minified and 36,624 gzipped. One call puts it
  on the shipped JPL DE440 pack:
  `import { useDe440 } from "@zodiacs/engine/de440/pack"; useDe440();`.
  [DE440 guide](../de440.md#the-default-source)
- **`require()` (breaking).** `require()` loads the package on Node
  `^20.19.0 || >=22.12.0`, the new `engines` range, and
  `ERR_ZODIACS_ESM_ONLY` is gone.
  [Runtimes](../guide.md#runtimes-and-module-formats)
- **Minor bodies.** `@zodiacs/engine/minor-bodies` places Chiron, Ceres,
  Pallas, Juno and Vesta from 1800 to 2200 TT. At 500 NASA JPL Horizons
  places the fit never saw, it is within 0.54″ after `useDe440()`.
  [Minor bodies](../guide.md#minor-bodies)
- **True Black Moon Lilith**, the osculating lunar apogee, in
  `chartPoints()` and `calc()`. At 12 instants from 1850 to 2150 it is
  within 1.59″ on the sky in longitude of Horizons's after `useDe440()`.
  [Points](../guide.md#points)
- **Fixed.** Timing results carry the fallback flags of the natal positions
  they use, and a search window that its conversion reverses is refused
  with an error that names the conversion and the step of the time basis.
- **Deprecated.** `@zodiacs/engine/core` is the root under its 2.x name.
- **Conformance.** On Node 22.22.2, suite 0.3.0: 639 of 640 vectors pass
  after `useDe440()` and 392 on astronomy-engine, the default; the
  unsupported case on both is a leap-second instant. Every verdict equals
  2.1.0's. [Conformance results](../../conformance/RESULTS.md)
- **Package size.** The archive holds 107 files, totaling 4,909,500 bytes
  unpacked. The package uses 1,419,948 of its 1,463,000 bytes beside the
  DE440 pack and the minor-body tables.
  [Size measurements](../evidence/3.0.0-20261127/results/package-size.json)
