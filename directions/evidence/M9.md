# M9 bounded scan evidence

This milestone adds the pure `scan` and `parseScan` APIs on top of the hit
implementation at checkout `4567020`. All implementation, fixtures, tests
and documentation remain inside `directions/`. The scan enumerates explicit
selections and motions on one common supplied sphere. It introduces no
traditional construction, ephemeris calculation or time-stepping search.

## Independent enumeration

[M9-derivations.md](M9-derivations.md) proves the synthetic input geometry,
every selected mundane contact's direct and converse arc, target hour angle,
and proportional position. [scan-examples.ts](scan-examples.ts) records the
authored table, independently ordered row indices and rational timing
multipliers. The fixture was written without importing or running any
directions implementation. Its solar samples are invented arithmetic inputs.

[scan.check.ts](../checks/scan.check.ts) compares every returned row with
this table under each supported key and year unit. Expected elapsed days
come from separate integer multiplication of the independently specified arc,
age multiplier and year unit. The test checks exact positive zero for the
initial contacts; the other numerical allowances are fixed in the fixture
and derivation before comparison. Additional hand arithmetic in the
derivation checks a zodiacal conjunction, dexter square and direct contacts
to the fixed axes. No input describes a person's natal chart.

The same tests compare each numerical example's returned hit with a separate
single-hit call. This establishes assembly parity only. It does not supply
the expected arcs or establish ephemeris accuracy. The pure import-graph
check in [hit.check.ts](../checks/hit.check.ts) continues to pass with the
scan exports reachable from the entry point.

## Selection and boundary policy

Each contact selection is crossed with each explicit motion. The shared
convention cannot contain a motion or converse construction. Contacts,
branches, coordinates, key, year unit, age endpoints, inclusion flags and
evaluation budget are all explicit. No implicit body pairs or recurrences
are added.

Tests enforce exact binary64 age comparisons without a tolerance, each
endpoint-inclusion combination, zero-width intervals, an exactly zero age,
and endpoints immediately adjacent to an exact representable age. A wide
age interval does not produce later full turns. Filtering concerns the
returned age; it does not certify astronomical error around an endpoint.

Canonical duplicates collapse before age filtering, preserving the first
selection/motion indices. Reordered object fields and negative zero do not
distinguish a request. Distinct labels, aspects and motions remain separate
even at equal ages. Results sort by age and then first enumeration order;
reordered motion lists exercise the tie rule. Conflicting original
coordinates under one body label are refused across selections, including
when the contacts would be outside the age interval.

## Work limits and failures

The required evaluation budget and exported implementation cap are checked
before any hit call. The test spies on the existing hit entry to verify that
over-budget requests evaluate nothing and do not read selected contacts.
The exact requested budget is accepted; exceeding it refuses with no
partial output. Duplicates and out-of-range contacts both count toward raw
work. Huge sparse arrays are rejected from their lengths without being
enumerated. The cap is a work-policy choice, not an accuracy or age limit.

An underlying typed refusal aborts the scan, discards every earlier row,
and records its original cause, failing indices and evaluated work. It
remains a refusal even if the requested age interval excludes all hits.
Unsupported combinations retain the single-hit API's existing restrictions.
Malformed structural input is distinct from mathematical hit refusal, and
budget refusal precedes validating the contact contents.

The checks reject omitted fields, unsupported values, cross-mode coordinates
or branches, irrelevant or missing solar data, nonfinite or reversed ages,
sparse arrays and unknown, inherited, hidden or accessor fields. Getter
spies confirm that accessor input is not invoked. Unexpected proxy failures
still propagate. These boundaries validate ordinary data; they are not a
sandbox for executable objects.

Complete and refused results are fresh, deeply frozen and JSON serializable.
Mutation checks change caller-owned coordinates, solar samples and endpoints
after calculation and verify that results stay unchanged. Input objects
remain unfrozen. [scan.types.ts](../checks/scan.types.ts) enforces the
required selections, mode/coordinate/contact correlations, explicit motion
construction, solar-key pairing, result discrimination and nested readonly
contracts. Prebuilt objects with motion fields in the shared convention are
also rejected by the type contract.
