# M8 complete hit evidence

This milestone assembles the existing sphere and timing primitives on the
directions checkout at `80fa047`. `hit` is the typed coordinate API and
`parseHit` accepts unknown data. `hitFromNatal` selects the coordinates from
an existing engine snapshot without making an ephemeris call. All remain
under the project's pure entry point. Engine preparation stays separate.

## Independent numerical checks

[M8-derivations.md](M8-derivations.md) derives complete synthetic arcs and
their directed ages. [hit-examples.ts](hit-examples.ts) contains the input
coordinates, expected arc, target hour angle, rational annual rate and
rational age. It imports no implementation and contains no earlier engine
or directions output. The sphere examples use the existing authored
geometrical identities, and the age expectations divide those arcs by the
independently specified timing rationals. Solar sample positions are
explicitly invented arithmetic inputs.

[hit.check.ts](../checks/hit.check.ts) compares complete hit results against
that fixture table with the allowances fixed in the derivation. It obtains
expected elapsed days from separate integer products of the rational age
and year unit. Zero expectations require exact positive zero. These checks
cover the listed hand-derived examples, not the ephemeris, a global error
bound or an event prediction.

The same checks verify that key or year-unit changes do not alter geometry,
that original labels survive converse role exchange, and that the ordinary
semi-arc construction applies uniformly to every label. Zodiacal angle
conjunctions preserve original latitude, explicitly project to zero, and
record the projection obliquity and resulting equatorial coordinates.
Body points on axes retain their body contact kind.

## Boundaries and data ownership

Missing or incompatible choices, mixed coordinate systems, cross-mode
aspect branches, contradictory coordinates under one body label, and
unsupported angular aspects are refused. The envelope preserves the
existing converse, pole, circumpolar and numerical-degeneracy refusals and
identifies their calculation stage. Invalid solar intervals and motion,
including at zero arc, and nonrepresentable timed ages remain refusals.
No refusal carries a partial hit.

Results are deeply frozen fresh data; tests mutate caller-owned nested
objects after calculation and verify that the result stays unchanged.
Inherited, hidden and accessor fields are rejected without invoking their
getters. Ordinary results and refusals round-trip through JSON. Unexpected
exceptions from executable inputs are not disguised as mathematical
refusals. [hit.types.ts](../checks/hit.types.ts) checks required choices,
mode/coordinate/contact pairing, solar-key requirements, stage-specific
codes and readonly nested output.

An import-graph check in `hit.check.ts` follows runtime imports from the
pure entry, excluding type-only declarations, and rejects external runtime
imports or engine/natal/solar calculation modules. The reachable modules
are reviewed pure coordinate, validation and arithmetic code. This is a
module-boundary check, not a general static proof of absence of side effects
in arbitrary future code.

## Engine integration

[natal-hit.check.ts](../checks/natal-hit.check.ts) verifies the data path
from `prepareNatal` through explicit body or angle selection to `hit`.
It compares with calls using the engine snapshot's coordinates directly.
It does not use those engine calculations as independent arc expectations.
Synthetic charts exercise each supported input clock and explicit or model
delta-T, both modes, each timing key, body and angle significators, and the
separation of natal house selection from direction system selection.

The adapter verifies consumed snapshot fields, including clock/place/frame
claims and agreement of selected ecliptic and equatorial source rows.
Missing or duplicate selected body names are refused; undefined RA cannot
be turned into zero. Unselected coordinates and unrelated metadata remain
unread. The returned natal audit copies geometry, time metadata, provenance,
house system, flags and selected original ecliptic coordinates or angular
longitude without retaining caller-owned objects.
[natal-hit.types.ts](../checks/natal-hit.types.ts) checks selection and
readonly audit contracts.

These checks do not authenticate a supplied snapshot or its provenance.
The snapshot does not contain a represented TT Julian date, so the helper
cannot bind solar samples to the natal instant. Solar hits explicitly
record `solarEpochAssociation: "caller-supplied-unverified"`. A test with
deliberately unrelated sample epochs enforces that limitation and the
preservation of the actual sample dates. Engine solar preparation and its
separate provenance remain available from M7.

## Scope

Supported successful contact kinds are conjunction or aspect to a body and
conjunction to an angle. Angle aspects remain typed refusals; no invented
angle-body stand-in enables them. The source register's zodiacal converse
and angular converse restrictions remain in force. No new traditional
convention, timing key, latitude policy, cusp, orbital motion or calendar
mapping is introduced. M9 and M10 retain the bounded scan and CLI work.
