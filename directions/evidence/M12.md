## Delivered guides and examples

[README.md](../README.md) now provides setup commands, supported scope and
navigation. The former detailed reference is retained in [API.md](../API.md),
with an entry-point map and runnable examples. [CLI.md](../CLI.md) adds an
output audit and a complete solar-sample handoff.
[CONVENTIONS.md](../CONVENTIONS.md) maps the source passages to supported and
refused combinations, distinguishes project choices from historical claims,
and works through the existing independent M8 sphere examples.
[SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) describes the current implementation rather than
future milestones; its source claims and reading records are unchanged.

[run-example.mjs](../scripts/run-example.mjs) bundles a named TypeScript
example into the ignored project build directory and prints JSON. Examples
are included in the local no-emit typecheck:

| Example | What is exercised | Evidence classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [pure-api.ts](../examples/pure-api.ts) | Coordinate hit and explicit direct/converse scan | Existing hand-derived M8 and M9 equatorial cases, checked by [examples.check.ts](../checks/examples.check.ts). |
| [natal-api.ts](../examples/natal-api.ts) | Engine natal preparation, body selection, fixed-key hit, scan and solar-key hit with retained sampling receipts | Integration only. The example explicitly pairs a TT chart label with the same TT Julian date; checks preserve this input relationship and the caller-asserted solar association. |
| [compare-techniques.ts](../examples/compare-techniques.ts) | Existing engine progression and longitude solar-arc helpers in a separate comparison | Integration only, checked by [comparison-example.check.ts](../checks/comparison-example.check.ts). No comparison output enters the primary solver or its numerical references. |

The pure example's hit arc and symbolic age are `20`, from
`(10 - 350) mod 360` on the zero-obliquity equatorial sphere. Its scan arcs
and ages are `90` and `270` from the direct and swapped conjunctions. The
independent references are [M8-derivations.md](M8-derivations.md), fixture
`zodiacal-wrap-ptolemy`, and [M9-derivations.md](M9-derivations.md), the
equatorial conjunction row. These are idealized coordinate cases, without
a natal date or an ephemeris accuracy claim.

The natal and comparison examples contain only invented charts. Their
tests compare wiring and serialized metadata with the existing public
engine helpers. They do not pin engine output as an expected accurate arc.
The solar CLI handoff is checked by [cli.check.ts](../checks/cli.check.ts):
the envelope emitted by the example produces the same complete hit through
the CLI, while the separate sampling provenance remains in the example
record. No new traditional source or numerical accuracy claim was introduced.
