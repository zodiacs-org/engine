# M7 timing-key evidence

This milestone adds pure `directedAge` and the separate opt-in
`prepareSolarEquatorialDay` engine adapter on the directions checkout at
`026b5e6`. Ptolemy, Naibod and the fixed birth-day solar-equatorial key are
explicit alternatives. Every conversion requires a year unit and retains
the input arc, symbolic age, annual rate and elapsed-day interpretation.
The fixed keys record their source IDs and exact defining rationals; the
solar key records both supplied RAs, TT Julian dates and the wrap branch.

## Independent arithmetic

[M7-derivations.md](M7-derivations.md) gives the source definitions, modern
solar policy, rational arithmetic and synthetic sample table.
[timing-examples.ts](timing-examples.ts) imports no implementation and uses
no earlier output as an expected value. The Naibod constant is the printed
decimal convention in Kühr's text, not a rate inferred from this engine.
[SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) and [source-readings.json](source-readings.json)
record the texts fetched and read and distinguish the sources' statements
from project choices.

[timing.check.ts](../checks/timing.check.ts) compares the rate and age with
independently derived rational expectations. Elapsed-day expectations use
separate integer rational products for both explicit fixed year units.
The fixture allowance is fixed in the derivation and source fixture;
zero expectations are exact. Synthetic solar cases exercise a forward
advance, a wrap through zero and a rate different from either fixed key.
These checks establish arithmetic agreement on the listed inputs. They
do not establish ephemeris accuracy, a global rounding bound or predictive
validity.

Additional checks reject missing or incompatible choices, non-data fields,
noncanonical arcs and RAs, intervals whose rounded difference hides a
residual, zero/reversed/ambiguous solar motion and overflowing or lost
positive ages. Zero arcs still validate every required convention and
sample. Results are independently allocated and deeply frozen without
modifying or retaining caller input. Compile checks in
[timing.types.ts](../checks/timing.types.ts) enforce the required choices,
solar-key discriminant and readonly results.

## Engine integration

[solar.check.ts](../checks/solar.check.ts) verifies that the opt-in adapter
uses the public calculation entry's solar positions and time resolution.
The input clock is explicit; the sampling day is always explicitly TT.
The adapter resamples the represented birth TT Julian date and the next
TT day. It retains the resolution receipt and each sample's receipt,
declination and engine bounds. Both samples keep the Sun's bodily latitude
and use their own true equator/equinox of date. No house cusp or longitude
is invented.

The invented integration instants cover each input scale, optional delta-T
pins, a UTC leap-second step, fractional Julian dates, engine range
refusals and a conversion through the pure timing API. These checks compare
with the engine's own public helper calls, so they establish data-path
preservation only. They do not supply independent solar positions or arcs.
[solar.types.ts](../checks/solar.types.ts) checks the adapter's explicit
clock and day selection and nested readonly provenance.

## Limits

The pure function accepts mathematical sample pairs, including synthetic
ones. It does not authenticate the Sun or impose an ephemeris date span.
The engine adapter preserves the calculation entry's range refusals for
the original resolution and both sampled dates, and does not substitute
another clock or ephemeris. Julian dates and positions retain the engine's
floating-point precision; a receipt is provenance, not an independent
accuracy guarantee.

The input arc is divided by the selected annual rate to obtain symbolic
age. The explicit year
unit changes only the fixed-day representation; no civil anniversary,
calendar date, secondary progression or longitude solar arc is computed.
Complete hit envelopes, bounded scans and CLI commands remain subsequent
milestones.
