# M5 zodiacal body direction evidence

This milestone adds `zodiacalBodyArc` on the directions checkout at
`a0cb49f`. It computes direct zodiacal conjunctions and classical aspect
rays to body significators using explicitly selected Placidus semi-arc
geometry and the project's `zero-both` projection policy. The result
contains the original inputs, prepared ecliptic and equatorial points,
semi-arcs, proportional transfer and first forward rotation. It returns
no age or complete hit envelope.

## Independent numerical evidence

[M5-derivations.md](M5-derivations.md) establishes the equations, source
orientation and authored arithmetic before comparing with the solver.
[zodiacal-examples.ts](zodiacal-examples.ts) imports no implementation.
Its solstice/equinox examples independently specify right ascensions,
declinations, semi-arcs, both initial hour angles, target hour angle and
directional arc. They cover both observer hemispheres, matching and opposed
declination signs, every quadrant and body significators at every axis.
The aspect examples separately establish dexter and sinister rays and the
unique opposition ray. Nonzero supplied latitudes remain visible while
their explicitly projected coordinates use zero latitude.

[zodiacal-evidence.check.ts](../checks/zodiacal-evidence.check.ts) compares
every recorded intermediate and arc with those expectations. The acceptance
allowance is the preregistered fixture constant explained in the derivation.
The equatorial arithmetic and policy checks in
[zodiacal.check.ts](../checks/zodiacal.check.ts) also establish rotation
orientation across RAMC and geographic latitude without an ephemeris.
No expected arc comes from this package's own earlier output, a personal
birth record or Swiss Ephemeris. These are finite analytic checks, not a
global error bound, astronomical accuracy measurement or predictive claim.

## Sources and limitations

The readings are recorded in [source-readings.json](source-readings.json)
and [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md). Placidus's canon XIX fixes the source of
each semi-arc term: carry the promissor's projected ray to the significator's
fixed proportional position. Lilly supplies the ray branch terminology.
The project projection does not implement changing significator latitude,
historical latitude-based effectiveness rules or the Sun's special
crepuscular construction. Every supplied body point follows the same
explicit semi-arc model above and below the horizon.

All zodiacal converse requests receive a typed refusal, including projected
conjunctions. The source's role swap is a mundane construction; this milestone
does not extend it to a zodiacal method. Angle targets, mundane contacts,
additional aspect sets and timing keys remain outside this solver.

The original sphere domain refusals propagate. Review also checked
longitude wrapping and loss of tiny separations during projection, hour-angle
subtraction and proportional transfer. Dedicated hand-derived arithmetic
checks preserve representable small arcs and require a numerical refusal
when the tested collapse would invent a zero contact or change quadrant.
No epsilon changes a boundary or selects a more convenient revolution.
Near-contact rounding can still affect the reported revolution outside
those detected cases; the implementation does not claim exact predicates.

## API and engine integration

Every convention, coordinate and contact choice is required. Runtime checks
refuse unknown, inherited, hidden and accessor fields at each input depth.
The exported TypeScript contract checks required choices and readonly
geometry. Results are fresh deeply frozen snapshots and do not mutate,
retain or freeze the caller's objects.

The solver reuses the engine's documented `eclipticToEquatorial` helper
through its pure source module, avoiding the root ephemeris import. A
synthetic natal integration check verifies coordinate and provenance flow
from the existing adapter and engine helper. That comparison is only an
integration check; it supplies no independent arc or ephemeris expectation.
