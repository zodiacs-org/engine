# M6 mundane body direction evidence

This milestone adds `mundaneBodyArc` on the directions checkout at
`b5f21cb`. It computes mundane conjunctions and classical aspects to body
significators under the explicit Placidus proportional semi-arc construction.
It retains both bodies' full equatorial coordinates. Direct moves the
promissor westward; converse requires the named role swap and moves the
significator westward. The result preserves original labels, contact branch,
initial sphere positions and intermediate target geometry. It returns no age.

## Independent geometry

[M6-derivations.md](M6-derivations.md) gives the sourced method, coordinate
orientation, branch relation and hand arithmetic.
[mundane-examples.ts](mundane-examples.ts) imports no implementation and
uses no earlier engine or directions result as an expectation. Its authored
inputs describe geometric points, not personal birth records. The examples
cover unequal diurnal and nocturnal semi-arcs, target horizon changes, both
observer hemispheres, every quadrant, both aspect branches, direct and
converse, and body points on exact axes. Exact equatorial initial aspects
are separate from oblique examples, where trigonometric input rounding can
affect the chosen revolution near coincidence.

[mundane-evidence.check.ts](../checks/mundane-evidence.check.ts) checks the
recorded hour angles, semi-arcs, proportional positions, target hour angles
and directional arcs. The acceptance allowance is the preregistered fixture
constant explained in the derivation. These are finite analytic comparisons,
not an ephemeris accuracy measurement or a global error bound. No Swiss
Ephemeris material or real birth data supplies an input or expectation.

The additional equatorial branch identities in
[mundane.check.ts](../checks/mundane.check.ts) verify the sign of each
contact before and after role exchange. The selected binary64 boundary
examples in [mundane-numerical.check.ts](../checks/mundane-numerical.check.ts)
are derived separately in M6-derivations. They exercise exact initial
contacts, tiny arcs, lower-meridian wrapping and refusals for detected
coordinate collapse. No tolerance snaps a contact to an axis or zero.

## Sources and limits

[source-readings.json](source-readings.json) and [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md)
record the fetched Placidus passages and locators. Canons XXX-XXXII
preserve bodily latitude and normalize the positions using the appropriate
horary times. Canon XXXVII exchanges the moving and fixed roles. The
aspect relation remains from the original promissor to the original
significator; `westward` and `eastward` explicitly name its direction in
the library coordinate, rather than silently borrowing zodiacal ray signs.

The uniform ordinary semi-arc construction excludes the author's special
solar crepuscular treatment, mundane parallels and minor aspects. This
body solver accepts no angular significator and does not extend converse
to angles. Sphere-domain failures propagate for either body instead of
substituting another system. Near-contact rounding can still affect the
chosen revolution outside the detected cases; no exact-predicate or global
numerical guarantee is asserted. Timing keys and the complete hit API
remain later milestones, and no predictive validity is claimed.

## Contract and integration

Runtime and compile checks require explicit conventions, coordinate fields
and meaningful contact branches. Malformed values, unknown fields and
accessors are refused. Results are fresh deeply frozen copies, with no
caller object retained, modified or frozen.

A synthetic natal integration check copies the adapter's engine right
ascension and declination rows and common geometry without projection. It
checks preservation of those values and natal provenance. It does not
supply an independent ephemeris or arc expectation. The mundane module
imports only project sphere code and convention types; it performs no
engine sampling or network access.
